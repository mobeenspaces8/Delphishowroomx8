Experience Design

12 Members

We design enterprise-grade product experiences that translate complex domain workflows into intuitive interfaces — combining research, systems thinking, and AI-native interaction patterns to accelerate adoption across our clients' organizations.

Design Frameworks We Own

Six battle-tested frameworks our team applies across enterprise UX engagements — refined through 50+ projects spanning healthcare, financial services, and government.

01.

Domain-First Research Loop

Embed with operators to map actual workflows. Interview 8-12 users per role, observe live sessions, and codify findings into a domain dictionary that anchors every design decision downstream.

02.

AI-Native Interaction Patterns

A library of 30+ patterns covering conversational interfaces, agentic flows, human-in-the-loop reviews, and trust-building micro-interactions. Each pattern ships with do/don't examples and code-ready specs.

03.

Enterprise Design System Kit

Token-driven foundation covering color, type, spacing, motion, and elevation. Built on auto-layout with variants for light/dark and density modes. Includes production-ready components with Code Connect mappings.

04.

Workflow Decomposition Canvas

A structured method to break a complex business process into atomic decision points, surface AI intervention opportunities, and prioritize based on volume, friction, and accuracy lift.

Best Practices We Push For Adoption

Operating principles every designer in the practice follows — refined through repeated client engagements and codified so new hires ramp fast and outputs stay consistent.

Start with the operator, not the org chart

Every project opens with a minimum 5 days of shadowing actual end-users. No persona is drafted without observed behavior to back it up.

Ship a clickable prototype within 2 weeks

Wireframes lose stakeholder attention. A working prototype — even rough — closes alignment loops 3x faster and surfaces edge cases earlier.

Design tokens before screens

Color, type, spacing, motion, and elevation are locked in week one. This prevents the late-stage scramble where 40 screens need re-tuning.

Accessibility is not a phase

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader semantics are reviewed in every weekly design crit — not bolted on at handoff.

Document the why, not just the what

Every Figma file has a decision log frame. Future designers and AI tools need to understand the reasoning behind a pattern to evolve it correctly.

Automated Patient Intake

Streamline patient onboarding with AI-driven data capture, smart routing, and seamless integration.

Try Demo View Prototype Watch Video

01. Executive Summary

An end-to-end automated intake solution designed to eliminate manual data entry, reduce wait times, and improve the overall patient onboarding experience leveraging advanced document intelligence and AI routing.

02. Problem

Healthcare facilities struggle with manual intake processes, leading to long waiting times, fragmented patient data, high administrative burden, and operational delays that negatively impact patient care.

03. Solution

Delphi AI improves the intake process through intelligent automation, AI-driven data capture from IDs and referral forms, smart routing to appropriate departments, seamless EHR integration, and journey optimization.

04. Outcome

Measurable business and patient-care impact including 60% faster processing times, improved patient experience scores, significantly reduced administrative effort, and near-perfect data accuracy in clinical records.

01

Patient Form Upload

Intake documents are securely uploaded via portal, email, or fax channels.

02

Document Ingestion

Automated ingestion pipeline digitizes and routes documents for processing.

03

OCR & Data Extraction

Advanced optical character recognition extracts raw text from varied formats.

04

Field Classification

AI models classify extracted text into specific patient and clinical fields.

05

Data Validation

Rule-based engine cross-references extracted data for accuracy and completeness.

06

Identity Verification

Patient identity is verified against existing master patient indexes.

07

Insurance Verification

Real-time API calls verify insurance eligibility and benefit coverage.

08

Exception Review

Flagged documents are routed to a human-in-the-loop dashboard for review.

09

System / EHR Entry

Validated structured data is pushed directly into the core EHR system.

10

Intake Completion

Patient intake workflow concludes, triggering next-step notifications.
